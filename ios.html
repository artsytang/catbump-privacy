CatBump Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CatBump ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

CatBump does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information in order to play.

The Game may store certain gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • game progress;
  • high scores;
  • virtual currency;
  • unlocked content;
  • game settings and preferences.

This information is used to provide and maintain the Game's functionality and is generally stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising

CatBump uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising, including rewarded advertisements that players may voluntarily choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers, subject to device settings, permissions, and applicable law;
  • advertising data, such as advertisements that have been shown;
  • interactions with the Game and advertisements;
  • crash logs and diagnostic information;
  • performance information.

Google may use this information for purposes such as advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and improving its services.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, including in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, CatBump may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be asked to make choices regarding the processing of personal data for advertising purposes.

You may also be provided with a way to review or change your privacy choices within the Game where required.

Advertising availability and personalization may differ depending on your choices and applicable legal requirements.

4. App Tracking and Device Identifiers

On iOS, access to certain advertising identifiers may depend on Apple's privacy requirements, your device settings, and permissions you provide.

CatBump respects the privacy choices and permissions provided through iOS and applicable consent mechanisms.

5. Third-Party Services

CatBump currently uses the following third-party advertising service:

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies, terms, and applicable legal requirements.

Google Privacy Policy:

6. Data Retention

Gameplay information stored locally on your device may remain until you reset the Game, delete its local data, or uninstall the Game.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is retained according to those providers' policies and applicable law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the Game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may provide you with rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, delete, restrict, object to, or withdraw consent for certain processing.

You may also use privacy controls provided by the Game, Google consent messages, or your device settings where applicable.

9. Children's Privacy

CatBump does not require an account and does not knowingly request directly identifying personal information from children through an account registration system.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been collected inappropriately, please contact us using the information below.
