CatBump Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CatBump ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play the iOS version of the Game.

1. Information We Handle

CatBump does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information to play the Game.

To provide the Game's features, certain gameplay information is stored locally on your device, including:

  • game scores and highest scores;
  • completed levels and game progress;
  • virtual coins and unlocked content;
  • cat ownership and equipment settings;
  • language, music, sound, and vibration preferences.

This information is used solely to provide and maintain the Game's functionality. It is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Local Data Storage

Gameplay information and settings are stored locally on your device using the device's application storage.

The iOS version does not provide online leaderboards, Game Center integration, cloud saves, or cross-device synchronization.

Your locally stored game data normally remains on your device until you delete the Game and its associated application data.

If you restore your device from an Apple backup, some application data may also be restored according to your device's backup settings.

3. Advertising and Tracking

CatBump does not display advertisements or integrate advertising services in this version.

We do not use Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or other advertising identifiers for advertising purposes.

We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell or share gameplay information for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

CatBump does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or performance-monitoring SDKs.

The Game does not contain an embedded web browser or load third-party web content during gameplay.

If you choose to open our Privacy Policy through the Game's settings, the link opens in your device's default web browser.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your gameplay information remains on your device and is managed locally by the Game.

You can remove your locally stored game data by deleting CatBump and its associated application data from your device.

We do not maintain a server-side account or database containing your game progress.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we will receive the information you provide and use it only to respond to your inquiry.

6. Children's Privacy

CatBump does not require players to provide personal information to use the Game.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game.

If you have concerns about personal information sent to us, please contact us using the information below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
